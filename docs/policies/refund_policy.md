# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval.

Digital goods (software licenses, downloadable content) are non-refundable
once activated, except where required by local law.

Subscription plans can be cancelled at any time; the customer retains access
until the end of the current billing period, but partial-month refunds are
not issued.

To request a refund, the customer must contact support with their order
number. Refunds over $500 require manager approval before processing.
