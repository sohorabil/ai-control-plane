# Account Security Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are never stored in plain text.
We support optional two-factor authentication (2FA) via authenticator app or
SMS.

If a customer suspects unauthorized account access, they should immediately
change their password and contact support. Support can force-expire all
active sessions for an account upon request.

Accounts are automatically locked after 10 failed login attempts within 15
minutes, and unlock automatically after 30 minutes, or immediately via a
password reset.

We never ask customers for their password via email, chat, or phone. Any
message requesting a password directly should be treated as a phishing
attempt and reported to support.
