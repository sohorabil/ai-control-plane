# Data Privacy Policy

We collect only the data necessary to provide our service: account email,
usage logs, and billing information. We do not sell customer data to third
parties.

Customers can request a full export of their data at any time by contacting
support. Data export requests are fulfilled within 10 business days.

Customers can request account deletion. Upon deletion, personal data is
removed from active systems within 30 days, though anonymized usage
statistics may be retained for analytics.

All customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Access to production
customer data is limited to engineers with an approved, time-boxed access
grant, logged in our audit system.
