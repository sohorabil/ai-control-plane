# Runbook: Billing Dispute

When a customer disputes a charge (e.g. "I was charged twice" or "I don't
recognize this charge"), follow these steps:

1. Ask for the order number, the date of the charge, and the amount in
   question.
2. Look up the transaction in the billing system. Confirm whether it matches
   an active subscription renewal, a one-time purchase, or appears to be a
   genuine duplicate.
3. If it's a confirmed duplicate charge, process a refund for the duplicate
   amount immediately — duplicate charges do not require manager approval,
   regardless of amount.
4. If the charge matches a subscription the customer forgot about, explain
   the subscription terms and offer to cancel future renewals if requested.
5. If the customer still disputes a charge that matches our records, offer
   to escalate to the billing team for a manual review, and set expectations
   that this may take up to 3 business days.

Do not promise a refund before confirming the transaction details — always
verify first.
