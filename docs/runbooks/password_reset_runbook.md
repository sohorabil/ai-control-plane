# Runbook: Password Reset

When a customer reports they cannot log in and believes it's a password
issue, follow these steps:

1. Verify the customer's identity using their account email and, if
   available, their order number or last billing date.
2. Ask the customer to use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
   This sends a reset link to their registered email, valid for 1 hour.
3. If the customer doesn't receive the email within 5 minutes, check that
   the email address on file is correct and ask them to check spam/junk
   folders.
4. If the account was locked due to failed login attempts, inform the
   customer it will unlock automatically in 30 minutes, or offer to force
   an immediate password reset instead.
5. If the customer still cannot reset after a valid link was sent, escalate
   to a Tier 2 support engineer — do not attempt to manually set a password
   for the customer.

Never ask the customer for their current or desired password over chat,
email, or phone.
